What it does

  • Dictation. Hold a shortcut, speak, let go. The text lands at your cursor, in any app. 25 languages with NVIDIA Parakeet; Whisper and other models for more.
  • Meetings. When Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Slack or FaceTime start a call, Sokki asks once. It records your microphone and the call apart, and removes echo.
  • Speaker names. Speakers are detected on the Apple GPU. Name a voice once; Sokki knows it next time.
  • The note. Summary, decisions, tasks with an owner and the full transcript, as Markdown in any folder. A 20 min call is ready about 3 min after you hang up.
  • Summaries. Gemma 4 E4B, built in and run on your Mac. Or Ollama. Or Claude, GPT or OpenRouter with your own key.
  • Notes by mail. After a meeting, Sokki can mail the note, never the transcript, to the people in it, from your own Mail account.
  • Autocorrect. Fixes typos as you type in apps where macOS doesn’t, such as Chrome and Slack. Off until you turn it on.
  • Command line. sokki transcribe, ask, search, sql, config and more, with JSON output. CLI.md
  • For AI agents. A Claude Code skill lets an agent search your meetings, transcribe files and ask the local model. No MCP server yet.

Questions

Does it work offline? Yes. After the models download once, everything runs without internet.

Is it a free alternative to Granola? It does the same job, meeting notes without a bot, on your Mac instead of a cloud, for nothing. Granola runs on more platforms and has an MCP server; Sokki has neither yet.

And to Superwhisper? On a Mac, yes. Parakeet, system audio and speaker separation are Pro in Superwhisper, at $8.49 a month. In Sokki they’re free. Why.

Can AI agents use it? Yes, through the sokki command and the Claude Code skill. What a cloud agent reads leaves your Mac; Sokki logs each read on your Mac.

Where’s my data? On your Mac. Every request that leaves.
