Your Mac. Open models. No bill.

Why Sokki costs nothing, and always will.

I liked Superwhisper. Then I wanted Parakeet: an open speech model from NVIDIA, free to download, running on my own Mac. Superwhisper puts it in Pro, at $8.49 a month, together with system audio, speaker separation and meetings.

A monthly fee to run a free model on my own computer didn’t sit right with me. So I built Sokki.

Superwhisper is a polished app, and it runs on more devices than Sokki does. This is about one thing only: open models on your own Mac should cost nothing.

Free and open source. For good.

No trial, no word limit, no account. Sokki runs on your Mac, not on our servers. The code is MIT, on GitHub, and it stays that way.

Build it with us.

Sokki is a fork of Handy by CJ Pais, and it grows the same way: in the open. Fix a bug, add a language, improve a model. Contributions are welcome: how to start.

What sponsoring pays for.

Sokki is built with AI coding tools. Sponsoring pays for the tokens. It opens soon; until then, a star on GitHub helps too.
