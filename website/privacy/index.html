Every request shows the server your IP address, as any connection does. Never sent: your name, your Mac’s name or serial number, your screen video.

Model downloads

When you pick a speech or language model, Sokki downloads it once from Hugging Face, or from Handy’s mirror (blob.handy.computer) if that fails. Then it runs offline.

Updates

A minute after start and every 4 hours, Sokki asks GitHub whether there is a new version. If there is, it downloads it, checks its signature and installs it after your first login of the day, when nothing is recording. Turn it off in Settings › Debug › Check for Updates.

Messages from Sokki

A minute after start and every 6 hours, Sokki downloads one small file from its counter (a Cloudflare Worker): how many people share, and a message from Sokki, if there is one. It sends nothing with it.

Help improve Sokki

Off until you turn it on. Then, under a random ID, once a day:

  • Sokki’s version, the models and languages you chose
  • your Mac’s model, chip, cores, memory, disk size and free space, macOS version and region setting
  • how many dictations and meetings you had, how long, in which language, how many speakers, which meeting app, which day
  • warnings and errors from Sokki’s log, with quoted words and your home folder blanked

With this or Share all my recordings on, at most hourly: how many of your own recordings you keep, and how many hours per language. Never audio, never anyone’s words.

Share all my recordings

Off until you turn it on. Then Sokki uploads every recording of your own voice on this Mac, unchanged, with its transcript. Recordings can contain names, numbers and what people near you said. Sokki may use them to improve Sokki and for open speech models, and may share or sell them.

Ask me when there’s a buyer

Off until you turn it on. Sokki compares buyer messages with the hours you keep, on your Mac. Nothing leaves until you answer I’m interested; then Sokki sends the random ID, the message, the language and the matching hours.

Phone link

Off until you turn on Accept recordings from my phone. Your phone then sends recordings straight to this Mac, end-to-end encrypted. To find the Mac, the link registers its public key with n0’s address service; when no direct connection is possible, n0’s relays pass the encrypted bytes along without being able to read them. Nothing is stored in between. The pairing code stays after the # in the sokki.org link, which browsers never send to a server.

Cloud language model

Sokki writes notes with a model on your Mac. If you pick Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter for meeting notes instead, the transcript goes to that provider. If you turn on dictation cleanup with a cloud provider (Advanced settings), the dictated text goes to it. You sign up with that provider and use your own key; its privacy terms apply.

Gmail

To send a meeting note to its attendees from your own address, Sokki asks Google for one permission: send email on your behalf (gmail.send). Sokki can’t read, search or delete your mail.

  • What Sokki does with it: sends the note you approve, from your Gmail, to the people you pick. Nothing else.
  • Where it’s kept: Google’s access token stays in your Mac’s Keychain. The token, your address and your notes never reach Sokki’s servers or anyone other than Google and your recipients.
  • Never: shared, sold, used for ads, or used to train AI models.
  • Disconnect: in Sokki’s settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sokki’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Slack

When you connect Slack, Sokki may post messages and see huddles in your workspace, so it can post a huddle’s note into its thread. Slack’s token stays in your Mac’s Keychain; the note goes only to Slack.

Calendar

With your permission, Sokki reads your Mac’s calendar through macOS to name meetings and know who attended. Calendar data stays on your Mac. Attendees’ addresses leave only in a note you send.

Delete everything

On the Your data page: delete your recordings, and Delete what was shared to remove everything the counter holds of your Mac; this also turns sharing off. Meeting notes are plain files: delete them like any file.

This website

No cookies, no fonts from other servers. Each page view adds one to a count: the page, the tag of the link that led there (like ?s=note) and the day. Nothing about you; the counter stores no IP addresses.

It’s hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Who and why

Responsible: RD Holding UG (haftungsbeschränkt), Loristraße 12, 80335 München, hello@sokki.org.

  • Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): Help improve Sokki, Share all my recordings, I’m interested, the phone link, cloud models and connected accounts. Turn the switch off or disconnect to withdraw it.
  • Our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in a working, safe, up-to-date app and website: model downloads, updates, messages from Sokki, the page count.

Who processes it: Cloudflare runs the counter and keeps shared data (Workers KV and D1) until you delete it; it counts writes per IP address in memory to stop abuse and stores none. GitHub hosts the website and the updates, Hugging Face and Handy’s mirror the models, n0 the phone link’s address service and relays. Cloudflare, GitHub and Hugging Face are US companies.

Your rights: ask what is stored under your Mac’s random ID, have it corrected or deleted, restrict it, take it with you, object to it, and complain to a data protection authority, such as the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht.
