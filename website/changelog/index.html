0.2.0

18 Nov 2026

  • Verbatim is now Sokki. Settings, meetings and permissions stay.
  • Fewer phantom speakers. On laptop calls the mic’s echo of the other side is removed before speaker detection. 3 speakers became the correct 2.
  • Your data. Your own recordings stay on your Mac. Two choices, both off: Help improve Sokki, and Support Sokki & Open Source.

18 Nov 2026

  • First release: meeting recording with call detection, speaker detection, local transcription, summary with decisions and tasks, Markdown notes, dictation.
