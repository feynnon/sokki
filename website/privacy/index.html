Privacy

Stays on your Mac.

What stays, what leaves, and how to check.

What stays

Meeting audioon your Mac, deleted after the period you choose
Transcripts and noteson your Mac, in the folder you choose
Your own recordingson your Mac until you delete them
Voices you namedon your Mac

Every request that leaves

Model downloadsonce per model, then offline
Cloud summaryonly if you pick Claude, GPT or OpenRouter: the transcript goes to that provider
Counter messagea few times a day, downloads one message, sends nothing
Help improve Sokkioff by default; once a day: usage counts, problems, hours per language, Mac model, macOS version
Support Sokki & Open Sourceoff by default; uploads your own recordings with their transcripts
“I’m interested”only when you answer a buyer message: a random ID, language, hours

Never sent: other people’s voices or words, your name, your Mac’s name. No account, no update checks. Recordings with a name, a number or contact details in their text stay on your Mac.

Check it: watch Sokki with a network monitor such as LuLu. The code is on GitHub.

Delete everything

On the Your data page: delete your recordings, and Delete what was shared to remove everything the counter holds of your Mac. Meeting notes are plain files: delete them like any file.

Permissions

Microphoneto hear you
System Audio Recordingto hear the other side of a call
Accessibilityto type dictated text into other apps

Recording people

Recording a conversation needs everyone’s consent. Tell people you’re taking notes.

This website

No cookies, no fonts from other servers. Each page view adds one to a count: the page, the tag of the link that led there (like ?s=note) and the day. Nothing about you; the counter stores no IP addresses.

It’s hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
